Section 01

Story

Chapter I — The Forgotten Tower

Long before the world forgot the old magic, deep inside the Whispering Woods, there stood a forgotten tower.

Its windows glowed green at midnight. Its walls were covered in ancient symbols, and strange whispers escaped through its crooked doors.

Chapter II — The Last Guardian

There lived a witch named Morgana, the last guardian of the Forbidden Grimoire.

For centuries, Morgana had protected the most powerful spell ever written: The Eternal Spell, a spell capable of restoring balance between the magical and human worlds.

But the spell came at a terrible cost.

It required ten rare ingredients, each carrying a different source of magical energy. Together, they could create a potion powerful enough to awaken the ancient magic sleeping beneath the world.

Chapter III — The Night the Magic Broke

One stormy Halloween night, Morgana began preparing the potion.

She opened the Grimoire, lit the candles, and placed her cauldron above the fire.

But as she spoke the final words of the ancient incantation, something went wrong.

The flames turned black.

The candles went out.

The Grimoire began to shake.

A dark force emerged from its pages and shattered the protective enchantment surrounding the tower. The ten ingredients vanished, scattered across a corrupted magical archive hidden deep within the tower's ancient mechanisms.

Chapter IV — The Grimoire Unprotected

When silence finally returned, Morgana discovered something even worse.

The Grimoire had lost its protection.

Without the ingredients, she could not complete the potion. Without the potion, she could not restore the protective enchantment. And without that protection, the dark force could return at any moment.

Chapter V — The Message Beyond the Woods

Morgana had one final choice.

She could not recover the ingredients alone.

So she sent a message beyond the Whispering Woods, searching for a new generation of witches and wizards.

The message was simple:

“My magic is fading. My ingredients are lost. The forbidden spell remains unfinished. If you possess the knowledge to repair what has been broken, enter my tower. Find my ingredients. Restore my magic. And whatever you do... do not let the Grimoire fall into the wrong hands.”

The tower doors opened.

Your trial begins now.

Frontend Design
